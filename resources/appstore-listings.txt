Test your Quran memorization

Now in 16 languages! Race a friend — or the honest bot «الحافظ» — in live 1v1 hifz matches, and climb the new cumulative monthly leaderboard.

Keywords:
hifz,hifdh,memorize,islam,muslim,koran,coran,hafiz,juz,surah,ayah,daily,game,learn,revision

Description:
Quran Quiz (اختبار القرآن) is a free multiple-choice quiz game that tests real Quran memorization (hifz).

Every question is generated from the actual text of the Quran (Uthmani script, ~77,900 words). Wrong answers are not random: they are drawn from genuinely similar, easily-confused verses (mutashabihat), so the quiz tests true recall — not guessing by familiarity.

FEATURES
• Personal study profile — the Quran is divided into 50 study parts. Mark the parts you have memorized, and questions are drawn only from them. Scores, per-part accuracy, and your daily streak are tracked.
• Rank ladder — climb from Beginner (مبتدئ) to Mujtahid (مجتهد), Hafiz (حافظ) and Mutqin (متقن) as your score grows, with milestone celebrations along the way.
• Daily quiz — one shared quiz for everyone, rotating every 24 hours, with a daily top list and a cumulative monthly leaderboard.
• Live 1v1 matches — challenge another player, or the honest bot "الحافظ", to a real-time race over the parts you both have memorized.
• Start instantly — play anonymously as a guest; sign in with Google or Facebook later to back up your progress and join the leaderboard by name.
• 16 languages — Arabic, English, Malay, Bengali, Turkish, Urdu, Punjabi, Persian, Hausa, Javanese, Pashto, French, Spanish, Indonesian, Chinese, and Swahili, with a right-to-left–first design and Quran text in the Madina Mushaf script.

Quran Quiz is free and also works in your browser at app.quranquiz.net.

What's New:

• The monthly leaderboard now adds up all your points across the month.
• Tapping a notification takes you straight to the right screen.
• Bug fixes and improvements.
